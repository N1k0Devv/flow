Delete your Flow account

Last Updated: November 3, 2026

Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Flow and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Data & reset (under Danger zone).
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Request deletion without the app

Email support@flowdaily.org from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my Flow account". We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your account (email, profile), tasks, habits and habit logs, goals, daily reviews, AI mentor sessions and memory, and stored Google Calendar connection tokens. Nothing is kept except records we are legally required to keep for payments (handled by Paddle). Cancel any active Paddle subscription separately via Settings → Manage subscription on desktop or the link in your receipt email.
