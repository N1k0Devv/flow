Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 3, 2026

1. Who we are

Flow ("FLOW", "we") is a productivity app for Windows, Mac and Android, and the website flowdaily.org, made by N1kodev. Contact: support@flowdaily.org.

2. Information we collect

  • Account: your email address (and name/avatar if you sign in with Google), used to sign you in.
  • Your content: tasks, habits and habit logs, goals, daily reviews, focus sessions and settings you create in Flow, synced so they appear on all your devices.
  • AI mentor: messages you send to the in-app mentor, plus the task/goal context needed to answer, and saved mentor memory and sessions.
  • Google Calendar (optional): if you connect it, the events Flow creates in your calendar and the OAuth tokens needed to keep the connection working.
  • Subscription status: whether your account has Flow Pro. Payment details are handled by Paddle; we never see your card number.
  • Website analytics: flowdaily.org uses Vercel Web Analytics (anonymous, cookie-free page-view counts). The apps do not include analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • On-device only: notification reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

3. How we use it

Only to provide Flow: sign-in, syncing your data across devices, reminders, AI mentor answers, calendar sync you turned on, and managing your subscription. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use it for advertising.

4. Service providers

  • Supabase - database and sign-in (stores your account and synced content).
  • OpenAI and Google (Gemini API) - process AI mentor messages to generate replies.
  • Google APIs - Google sign-in and Calendar sync, only if you use them.
  • Paddle - payment processing for purchases on desktop and web.
  • Vercel - website hosting and anonymous website analytics.

We may disclose limited data if required by law or to investigate abuse, fraud or security incidents.

5. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and stored with Supabase, which encrypts data at rest.

6. Retention and deleting your account

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account and all synced data at any time in the app: Settings → Data & reset → Delete account. You can also request deletion on the web at flowdaily.org/delete-account. Deletion removes your account, tasks, habits, goals, reviews and mentor history from our servers. Any active Paddle subscription should be cancelled separately.

7. Google user data

Flow's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Flow requests only https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events (to create, read, update and delete events Flow authors on your behalf) and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.profile (your name and avatar). We do not sell Google user data, share it with advertisers, or use it to train AI models.

8. Children

Flow is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Your rights and changes

You can access, correct or delete your data in the app or by emailing support@flowdaily.org. We will post updates to this policy on this page.
